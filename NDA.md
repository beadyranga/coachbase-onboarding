This agreement is between **[Ranga Amesh Wijekoon]**, the CEO of CoachBase (the
"Owner"), and **you**, any person who writes, edits, reviews or submits code
or other material to the CoachBase repository (a "Contributor").

**You must sign this agreement before you are given access to any CoachBase
code.** You sign it by opening a signature issue in this repository (see the
end of this file). Your typed full legal name, GitHub username and the date
are your electronic signature. It covers every contribution you make.

CoachBase and all of its intellectual property, including source code,
designs, databases, documentation, product ideas, names and logos (the
"Project IP"), belong to the Owner. Until a written partnership or
co-founder agreement says otherwise, **no one other than the CoachBase CEO and
the partners he appoints in writing has any legal right to the code**,
including the parts they wrote themselves.

## 2. Your contributions belong to the Owner

1. Anything you create for CoachBase, or using its code or information, is a

   documents and ideas.
2. Each Contribution belongs to the Owner from the moment it is created. To

   and interest in it, worldwide, for the full term of those rights, with no
   payment.
3. You waive, or agree not to assert, any moral rights in your Contributions,

4. When the Owner forms a company for CoachBase, the Owner may transfer the
   Project IP and this agreement to it. You agree to sign any document
   reasonably needed to confirm that.
5. This continues to apply **until the Owner and you sign a separate written
   partnership or co-founder agreement**, and then only to the extent that
   agreement changes it.

## 3. Confidentiality and no other use

While you contribute and **at any time afterwards**, you must not, without the
Owner's written permission:

1. use CoachBase code or information for any purpose other than working on

2. copy it into, or reuse it in, any other product, for yourself or anyone

3. sell, license, publish or share it with anyone, including in a public or

4. claim ownership of, or register any right in, any Project IP.

Everything in this repository is confidential unless the Owner has made it
public.

change and distribute it as part of CoachBase. Third-party or open-source code
may be added only if its licence allows commercial use.

## 5. When you stop contributing

When you stop contributing, or when the Owner asks, you will within 7 days
delete every copy of CoachBase code and information you hold, hand back any
credentials, and accept that your access will be removed.

## 6. No employment, partnership or equity

This agreement does not make you an employee, partner or agent of the Owner,
and gives you no shares, equity, profit share or payment. Any of those

## How to sign

1. Read this agreement in full.
2. Open the **Sign the CoachBase NDA** issue form in this repository
   (Issues → New issue).
3. Tick the acceptance box, type your full legal name, GitHub username and
   today's date, and submit.
4. Once your signature is checked, the CoachBase CEO will invite you to the
   private CoachBase repository.
